# Legend — Brand Identity

## Logo
- Gold armored warrior standing on a cliff edge, sword drawn
- Flowing tattered cape (motion, power, legend in the making)
- Crown symbol above the wordmark
- "LEGEND" in gold serif spaced capitals with a horizontal rule

## Colors
- Primary: #C9A84C (gold)
- Secondary: #F5D78E (light gold / highlights)
- Dark: #8B6914 (deep gold / shadows)
- Background: #000000 (pure black)

## Typography
- Wordmark: Wide-spaced serif capitals
- UI: Clean sans-serif on dark backgrounds

## Tone
- Powerful. Timeless. Built to last.
- Not a tool — a force.

## Usage
- Place logo on black or very dark backgrounds only
- Minimum size: 120px wide
- Do not distort, recolor, or place on light backgrounds
