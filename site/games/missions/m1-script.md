# Mission 1 — "Burning Sky"

Colombia, 1999. Mission: classified. Objective: investigate.

## The team

| Name | Who | Look | Carries | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Carlos "Spite" Martinez** (you) | Gets the job done; loves his team | Civilian clothes under a black plate carrier | M4 with red dot, M1911 in a holster | Even, dry |
| **Payback** | 30. Loud, curses a lot, proud of her work. Eyes that have seen everything | Civilian clothes under a plate carrier, long face mask, skull tattoo on her shoulder, no helmet | Bronze Desert Eagle (primary) | Sharp, fast |
| **Molotov** | Russian-American. Strict, confident, almost never speaks. Nobody knows his real name; nobody has seen his face | Black ski mask spray-painted with flames (orange, red, yellow), black around the eyes, pitch-black tactical gear | Pump shotgun with a fire charm, Molotovs | Low, flat |

## 1. Cold open — the helicopter (cutscene)

Sunset. A helicopter comes in low over burning hills. Inside, nobody speaks:

- **Payback** rolls her shoulder; the skull tattoo flexes.
- **Mike** taps at a tablet, then stops and stares at nothing.
- **Molotov** watches the fires through the open door, the fire charm swinging from his shotgun.
- **Alec** checks an old wristwatch and smiles to himself.
- **Spite** looks round at each of them.

Wide exterior: the helicopter flares and settles on a rooftop. Two armoured soldiers stand by the pad. Bottom-left, typed in:

> COLOMBIA, 1999
> MISSION: CLASSIFIED
> OBJECTIVE: INVESTIGATE

## 2. The rooftop (cutscene)

Sergeant Lincoln walks up and shakes Alec's hand.

- **LINCOLN:** Sharp. Took your sweet time.
- **ALEC:** I'm eighty-two, Sergeant. Everything takes its sweet time.
- **LINCOLN:** We gotta get the job done. Investigate what's going on down there.

He points. Wide shot over the parapet: smoke, rubble, a city engulfed in flames.

- **MIKE:** What... what *is* going on down there?
- **LINCOLN:** That's what you're here to find out.

A mortar round slams into one of the tanks in the street below. Everyone ducks.

- **PAYBACK:** Holy shit! That was a tank!
- **BRIAN WOLFHARD** *(prone behind his .50)*: Mortar team, two klicks east. Can't reach 'em from here.
- **JESSE WOLFHARD:** Speak for yourself.
- **BRIAN:** I *am* speaking for myself, Jesse.
- **LINCOLN:** Stairwell's behind you. Move.
- **MOLOTOV:** Move.

## 3. Down through the building (play)

Each floor has soldiers posted, an ammo crate and a map. Picking up the map is optional intel.

- **4th floor.** *SOLDIER:* Ammo's on the table, take what you need. *(map)* **ALEC:** The street runs north to the plaza. They're dug in all the way along it.
- **3rd floor.** *SOLDIER (on the radio):* ...say again, how many? ...Copy. *(to the team)* They just keep coming.
- **2nd floor.** A medic over a wounded man. *WOUNDED SOLDIER:* They didn't look like soldiers. Jeans. Sandals. And they fought like they had nothing left to lose.
  **MIKE:** That's... that's not great.
- **Ground floor.** **ALEC:** Right then. Nice and quiet... is what I'd say if I were a liar.

## 4. The street (play) — four waves, thirty hostiles

- **Wave 1:** **PAYBACK:** Contact front! Come and get some, you bastards!
- **Wave 2:** **MIKE:** I'm not killing anybody I don't have to! **PAYBACK:** Then shoot faster, Mike!
- **Wave 3:** **ALEC:** Steady now. Pick your shots, lad.
- **Wave 4:** **SPITE:** Last of them, push up!

## 5. Bird down (cutscene into play)

A friendly helicopter crosses overhead, takes a hit, spins and crashes in the street ahead.

- **SPITE:** Bird down! Bird down!
- **ALEC:** Into the wreck! Use it!

Rounds zip off the fuselage.

- **PAYBACK:** Snipers in the windows. Six of the sons of bitches.

She slaps a sight onto her bronze Desert Eagle and hands it over.

- **PAYBACK:** Here. Optic's zeroed. You scratch her, I scratch you.
- **JESSE WOLFHARD** *(radio)*: No angle on those windows from up here. They're yours, Spite.

**Objective:** kill the six snipers. As each falls, the squad calls the next window. When they're down:

- **PAYBACK:** That's my girl. Hand her back.

## 6. Hydra (play into cutscene)

- **ALEC:** Movement on the hill. Spite, have a look.

Binoculars. Black tanks crest the hill, white letters on their sides: **HYDRA**.

- **SPITE:** Tanks. Black ones. They've got... "Hydra" painted on them.
- **MIKE:** Hydra? That's not a unit. That's not anybody's unit.

Ten seconds after they appear, they open fire.

- **PAYBACK:** Oh, you have *got* to be shitting me!

**Cutscene:** shells walk up the street. Spite sprints for a mortar knocked on its side, is nearly hit twice, drags it behind a wall, stands it up and drops a round in.

- **ALEC:** That's my boy!

## 7. The mortar (play) — six tanks

Aim the mortar; the squad spots for you.

- **MOLOTOV:** Left. Fifty.
- **BRIAN** *(radio)*: Good hit! Good hit!
- **JESSE** *(radio)*: You missed one, genius, it's still moving.

When the sixth tank burns:

- **ALEC:** That's the last of them!
- **EVERYONE:** Hooah!

Fade to black.

## 8. Debrief (cutscene)

The mission office. Everyone at the table; Molotov in the corner. Lincoln drops a folder on the desk.

- **LINCOLN:** Hydra. A force we have never seen before. No flag. No country. No records.

He fans out photographs: the tanks, and the men you fought.

- **LINCOLN:** Three weeks ago these were civilians. Farmers. Shopkeepers. One of them taught primary school.
- **MIKE:** We... I...
- **PAYBACK** *(quietly)*: Jesus Christ.
- **ALEC:** Then somebody turned them. Somebody handed a schoolteacher a tank.
- **MOLOTOV:** Why were we there?

Silence. Lincoln stutters.

- **LINCOLN:** We... the... it was a — civilians requested us to come there.

Nobody speaks. Every one of them narrows their eyes.

- **LINCOLN:** Dismissed.
- **MIKE:** Sir, with respect, if civilians requested us, then who was it we were —
- **LINCOLN** *(pointing at the door)*: **DISMISSED!**

The team files out. Fade to black.

## 9. Mission passed

Upbeat music. **MISSION PASSED**, then stars and completion.

**Stars (3).** One star off for dying more than three times. One off for any friendly fire.

**Friendly fire.** Shooting a teammate cuts straight to black: *FRIENDLY FIRE WILL NOT BE TOLERATED*. You go back to the last checkpoint.

**Completion %:**
- 50: the objectives.
- 20: the intel maps (one per floor).
- 15: every hostile cleared.
- 15: no cutscene skipped.

**Buttons:** Restart mission · Next mission · Main menu.
