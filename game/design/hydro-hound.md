# Hydro Hound — HYDRA Robot War-Dog

> **Canonical tech/bestiary entry.** Specs transcribed from the official concept
> sheet: `game/design/assets/hydro-hound-leather.png` (shown in the LEATHER
> skin). Deployable in **Multiplayer (Power slot)** and **Zombies.**
>
> **Official name: "HYDRO HOUND"** (per the sheet, under the HYDRA brand — the
> hexagonal three-headed-serpent badge is stamped on its armor).

## What it is
A **robot Doberman made of metal and wires** — HYDRA-built. Fast, loyal,
permanently deployed, lethal at every range.

## Specs (from the sheet)
| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Height | **32 in** |
| Length | **48 in** |
| Weight | **120 kg** |
| Max speed | **35 mph** (slightly faster than a sprinting player) |
| Power core | **HYDRA CELL MK.II** |
| AI unit | **HOUND OS** |

## Features
- Enhanced sensors · Target acquisition · **Silent servos**
- **Neurotoxin bite** · **Dual machine guns** · Flexible leather plating
- Integrated comms · Night vision · All-terrain paws

## Weapons
- **Dual mounted machine guns** — **2 × 7.62mm, belt-fed, 360° arc fire** (the
  side turrets).
- **Neurotoxin bite** — injects a **fast-acting neurotoxin causing paralysis,
  disorientation, and respiratory failure.**

## Gameplay (Power slot — see modes/multiplayer.md)
- **Permanent deployment** — never expires; only leaves the map when destroyed.
- **Leap attack** within ~3 ft → mauls the target to death.
- **Heels within 10 ft of owner**; breaks heel the moment it spots an enemy and
  **hunts them until dead.**
- **Health:** ~4 players' worth; **Armor:** ~15 players' worth of semi-auto
  AK-47 fire; armor significantly reduces grenade/fire/EMP/bullet damage.
- **Traversal:** leaps small drops; big falls → **mini parachute**, glides down
  beside you.

## REACTIVE SKINS (evolve mid-match)
From the **Reactive Skin: GOLD** sheet (`assets/hydro-hound-reactive-gold.png`):
- **Unlock:** earn the **base (Gold) skin** first → unlocks its **Reactive**
  version. Equipped at match start; **evolves as you earn kill streaks WITH the
  Hydro Hound.** *"Each stage reflects your dominance on the battlefield."*

| Stage | Look | Trigger | Sheet text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — **DEFAULT** | Standard black armor | Match start | "Standard Hydra Hound armor. Built for speed. Built to kill." |
| 2 — **GOLD** | Gold-plated armor | **3 kill streak** | "A sign of domination and superiority." |
| 3 — **COSMIC** | Infused with cosmic particles (glowing purple, toxin-drooling fangs) | **5 kill streak** | "Its bite becomes **even more lethal**." *(functional boost!)* |
| 4 — **AURORA BOREALIS** | Aurora-powered iridescence | **8 kill streak** | "A rare and beautiful force of destruction." |

- Implication: there's a **base Gold skin** in the catalog (between Fire@30 and
  Broken Spirit@100? unlock TBD), and reactive variants may exist for other
  skins too.
- **Stage 3 is a functional evolution** (more lethal bite) — reactive stages
  aren't purely visual.
