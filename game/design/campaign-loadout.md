# Campaign Loadout System — The Mission Board

> **Canon (creator-specified), with unlock pacing DELEGATED to the designer**
> (the creator asked me to choose *after which mission* each item unlocks —
> those picks are marked **[D]** and are adjustable on request; the allowed
> item lists themselves are creator canon).

## The System
- **Before every mission**, at the **MISSION BOARD**, the player chooses:
  1. **PRIMARY**
  2. **SECONDARY**
  3. **LETHAL**
  4. **MELEE** *(⚠ "Malay weapon" = melee. The creator has NOT yet listed
     melee options — knife assumed as default; list TBD.)*
- Weapons/attachments **unlock as the campaign progresses** — you don't get
  the full list up front.
- **Mission 1 exception:** **no choice.** You are issued a **standard-issue
  M4 with IRON SIGHTS and a LASER.** (The board doesn't exist yet —
  you're a soldier being handed a rifle.)
