## Design Notes

- **Payback's mechanic:** she has no fixed weapon. Her entire character concept
  is battlefield improvisation — whatever the enemy drops, she picks up. Every
  weapon on this list is a potential Payback loadout.
- **Fox's 40 years:** his experience spans weapons from every era on this list.
  He's held a Sten, a Bren, and a SPAS-12. He picks the M3 shotgun because it's
  the best close-in tool for leading men through buildings.
- **Molotov's M16:** standard weapon, non-standard cosmetic — the flame charm
  is the only personalisation he allows himself.
- **The anti-tank rifle in Mission 1** is the Barrett M82 (#108) — the snipers
  on the Clock Tower had an anti-materiel rig pointed down at people, which is
  what Payback notices.
- **HYDRA weapons** are deliberately anachronistic and advanced — they should
  *feel* wrong in a 1985 setting, because they are.
