## BEAT SHEET (every detail, in order)

### Framing (1985 boardroom)
1. Screen desaturates to **black & white**, bleeding into an **office/boardroom**.
2. The **team is yelling around the table at the President** and other corporate
   figures. **Spike daydreams in the corner.**
3. President asks Spike: **"You saw the tanks directly. What were they?"** — no
   response.
4. President: **"Are you daydreaming over there?"** Spike: **"Just thinking."**
5. President presses; Spike repeats **"Just thinking,"** quietly, once more.
6. Camera **slowly zooms into Spike's head** → opens onto the flashback.

### Flashback (Saudi Arabia, August 14, 1948)
7. **Jeep insertion** — an unnamed soldier (Diego) rides in with **three others**
   armed with **AK-47s / period weapons** (⚠ see era flags).
8. The target — a **small city** — is **walled off by HYDRA barriers** and its
   **gate guarded by HYDRA soldiers**.
9. The squad dismounts. **"The Thirty":** *"We doing this or not, Diego?"*
   Diego nods: **"I'm always ready."**
10. Diego **grabs a grenade and throws it at the HYDRA gate**, blowing it open.
11. **Firefight: ~20 enemy soldiers outside the gate, ~30 more inside.** Wipe
    them all out.
12. Head into the **main center building.** The two flanking men are named here:
    **Park** and **Russell.** Diego **blasts the door off its hinges.**
13. Inside: a **HYDRA scientist** + several guards. **Mexican standoff** — every
    weapon up.
14. The scientist opens a **laptop/terminal** with a **LAUNCH** button and
    **threatens to press it** (⚠ see era flags).
15. **The scientist's own guards turn their guns on HIM:** *"We've all got
    someone back home to get to. You don't have to do this."*
16. The scientist watches their fingers tighten — **presses LAUNCH** — and is
    **shot down instantly.** Too late.
17. Everyone **bursts outside.** A **rocket launches into space**; at apex
    **~13 objects detach** and scatter, **landing in different directions**
    across the world.
18. **One capsule lands right next to Diego.** Everyone **collapses**, then wakes
    — **screen ringing like a concussion.**
19. The capsule opens: a **HYDRA drone** unfolds. It **effortlessly tears apart
    and neutralizes a guard.** Diego doesn't watch.
20. The team tries to run; **two more drones land in front of them.** **Park and
    Russell try to fight — both are killed.**
21. **"The Thirty" tells Diego to RUN.** Diego runs.
22. The Thirty **looks back one last time and detonates a Claymore** (⚠ era
    flag) — **the entire facility erupts in flame.**
23. Fire bleeds back to color → **the 1985 boardroom.** Spike is still lost in
    thought. The President **says his name a few more times** until he **finally
    snaps out of it.**

## MAJOR LORE (canon — do not forget)

- **HYDRA has existed since at least 1948** — 37 years before the 1985 campaign.
- **The 1948 Launch:** a HYDRA scientist launched a **rocket into space** that
  deployed **~13 objects/capsules** worldwide, **scattering in different
  directions.** These capsules contain **HYDRA drones** (machine-creatures).
  This is almost certainly the **origin/seeding event** of the threat seen in
  1985 — a planet-wide planting of HYDRA assets.
- **HYDRA drones** are fast, brutal, and capable of effortlessly killing trained
  soldiers. First deployed from these capsules in 1948.
- **HYDRA's own people are not all true believers** — the scientist's guards
  turned on him ("we've all got someone back home"). HYDRA loyalty vs. coercion
  is a usable theme.

## ⚠ ERA / TECH FLAGS (1948 setting — for a deliberate decision)

The user said "AK-47 **or just whatever historically accurate things existed at
that time**," so flagging the period mismatches so we can choose per item:

1. **AK-47 in Aug 1948** — the AK-47 was adopted **1949**, just after this date.
   Period-correct 1948 options: **StG 44**, **M1 Garand / M1 Carbine**,
   **Thompson M1928**, **PPSh-41**, **Mosin-Nagant**, **Lee-Enfield**, **MP40**.
2. **Laptop/terminal in 1948** — laptops didn't exist. *However,* HYDRA already
   wields rockets and drones, so this reads as **intentional advanced HYDRA
   tech** (alt-history sci-fi). Keep as a sleek HYDRA terminal, or reframe as a
   period control console — your call.
3. **Claymore in 1948** — the M18 Claymore came ~**1960**. Period stand-ins:
   **satchel charge**, **dynamite bundle**, **TNT/grenade cluster**, **demo charge**.
4. **HYDRA drones + space launch in 1948** — wildly ahead of the era, but clearly
   **intentional alt-history sci-fi** and core to the lore. Keeping as-is.

Default: recorded **as told**, with these flags noted, until you decide.
