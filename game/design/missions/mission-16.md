## OPENING — THE FAMILY COUNCIL (Molotov, Elizabeth, Jonah)

> *(Night. A quiet corner of the renovated warehouse — the Newman family's
> quarters. ELIZABETH sits with a mug going cold. JONAH sprawls nearby,
> field-stripping his rifle out of habit. MOLOTOV stands at the window, mask
> off in the dark — only his family gets this. His voice, as ever, is flat and
> quiet. But the words aren't.)*
>
> **MOLOTOV:** ...I don't know that we're doing the right thing.
>
> *(Elizabeth doesn't startle. She just waits. She's fluent in his silences.)*
>
> **MOLOTOV:** The plan is the door, the palace, the trigger. Kill Hunter.
> *(beat)* I keep thinking about the other math.
> **JONAH:** What other math?
> **MOLOTOV:** Hunter wants Prestige dead. So do we. *(beat)* What if we side
> with him. Take Prestige down together. And through Prestige... we get to
> Abraham.
>
> **JONAH:** *(sitting up)* Dad — he said he'd *take one of us.* We heard him.
> **MOLOTOV:** I heard him.
> **JONAH:** And you'd still work with him?
> **MOLOTOV:** I'd use him. *(beat)* There's a difference. Usually.
>
> **ELIZABETH:** *(quiet, even)* Webber never asks questions out loud.
> *(to Jonah, gently)* Your father's been deciding this for days. He's not
> asking us what to do. He's asking us to forgive him if he's wrong.
>
> *(Silence. Molotov doesn't correct her. That's how they know she's right.)*
>
> **ELIZABETH:** *(to Molotov)* You watched a good man die because a general
> smiled at the right people. So you don't trust anyone who smiles. Hunter
> doesn't smile at you. Maybe that's why you trust him enough to consider it.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(long pause)* ...He's cruel.
> **ELIZABETH:** So is winter. You still walk through it to get home.
>
> **JONAH:** *(quiet now)* ...What happens if you're wrong?
> **MOLOTOV:** *(finally turning from the window)* Then I'll be wrong once.
> And he won't get a second time.
>
> *(— SCENE. The question stands: bring it to the team? —)*

## THE DECISION — MOLOTOV GOES ALONE
- **Resolved: Molotov agreed to go talk to Hunter ALONE.** The Assault becomes,
  first, a **parley of one.**
- **The basis — the parallel between the two men:**
  - **Both have families they love** (for different reasons).
  - **Both want Prestige dead.**
  - Two fathers, two legacies: Molotov passes down a **fire mask**; Hunter, a
    **criminal empire.** Each man will recognize himself in the other — that's
    the gamble.
- **Hunter's family (canon, see GAME_DESIGN):** three children (had them by age
  20) — his heirs; three trusted soldiers sworn to become their right hands when
  his time comes. A man obsessed with succession, "as soon as possible."
- *(Note the charge on this meeting: Hunter threatened to "take one of" the
  team — and the man walking in alone is the one whose wife and son live at
  HQ.)*
