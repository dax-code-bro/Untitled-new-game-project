## Title Meaning (foreshadow — DOUBLE meaning)
"Hellfire" pays off three ways at once:
1. The **carpet-bombing inferno** of HYDRA's H2.1 bombers.
2. The literal **AGM-114 Hellfire missiles** the President orders fired at the
   swarm.
3. The **rain of burning fireballs** — dead drones falling as shrapnel-fire onto
   the city. Hellfire from above, paid for in blood.

## BEAT SHEET (opening — every detail, in order)
1. **Immediate launch.** No HQ scene. The team is already on **helicopters and
   planes**, scrambling toward **Texas.**
2. **The "why," realized in transit.** They figure out why Texas + several states
   are being carpet-bombed: the **state government is hosting its annual dinner at
   the Texas town hall** — every official in one room. **That's the real target.**
3. **Why bomb everything en route.** The **H2.1 drones must empty their entire
   bomb payload before they can self-destruct.** So they **carpet-bomb 7+ states
   on the way to Texas** just to expend their bombs and detonate on the dinner.
4. **The counter — and its failure.** The team deploys **EMP Sharks**. The drones
   are electrical, but **too powerful** — even a full Shark blast can't stop them.
5. **The no-win call.** Abraham puts the **President & government** on an
   emergency line. Two options: **let the drones pass (kill ~billions)** or fire
   **UAV-launched Hellfire missiles** (each volley kills ~¼ of the swarm,
   repeatable) — but the dying drones become **burning shrapnel fireballs** that
   kill **hundreds/thousands** below. **No way to win.**
6. **The order.** The **President chooses Hellfire** over Abraham's objection.
7. **Hellfire deployed.** From the Chinook Spike flies, missiles tear the swarm
   apart. Everyone celebrates — except Abraham.
8. **The cost.** Epic music; the streets fill with people looking up as hundreds
   of burning fireballs rain down like deadly fireworks.
9. **The fall.** Drones strike the Chinook (one, then another, then another); it
   crashes. Black → concussion-ring → **Spike's POV**, rubbing his head.
10. **Through the city.** The team stumbles out, dodging fireballs, deeper into
    the city to **City Hall.**
11. **Extract the President.** They grab him; his guards are killed by drones
    crashing into the hall. Out through alleyways to find an escort vehicle.
12. **The drive.** A **luxury car** through the burning city; blocked by rubble;
    help the President over it on foot.
13. **Escape.** Clear of the drones, load the President onto a helicopter with the
    team and get out.

## FULL SCRIPT

### COLD OPEN — STRAIGHT INTO THE AIR

> *(NO briefing. NO HQ. Hard cut from the alarm to the SKY: a fleet of
> HELICOPTERS and PLANES already screaming toward Texas, the team aboard, gearing
> up mid-flight. These are special **gas- and biofuel-powered** craft — no
> electrical engines — chosen so the team can fly through their own EMP blast
> unharmed.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** Somebody tell me why Hydra's turning seven states into a graveyard!
> **ABRAHAM:** *(over comms)* Because they're not after the states. Texas is
> hosting the annual state government dinner — tonight, the town hall. Every
> official in one room. *That's* the target.
> **FOX:** Then why bomb everything between here and there?
> **ABRAHAM:** The H2.1s are rigged to self-destruct — but they can't blow until
> they've dumped their **full payload** first. They have to empty every bomb
> they're carrying. So they carpet *everything* on the way down. Seven states.
> Just to die on top of that dinner.
> **SPIKE:** ...That's insane.
> **ABRAHAM:** That's Hydra.

### THE COUNTER — EMP

> **ABRAHAM:** We're deploying **EMP sharks** — fry their guidance, drop them out
> of the sky before they reach a population center. *(beat)* Get to work.

### THE EMP FAILS

> *(The Sharks open their latches and unload — thousands of EMP bomblets across
> the swarm. The H2.1 bombers flicker... and keep flying. They're electrical, but
> they're drawing too much power; even a full Shark blast barely tickles them.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** They're — they're still coming?! That was a *full blast!*
> **ABRAHAM:** *(grim)* ...It wasn't enough. Nothing we've got is enough.

### THE NO-WIN CALL (emergency line to the President)

> *(Abraham gets the President and the government on an emergency line.)*
>
> **ABRAHAM:** Mr. President — the EMP's a wash. The H2.1s draw too much power; a
> full Shark blast barely touched them. They're all still inbound.
> **PRESIDENT:** *(over the line)* ...Then what are my options, General?
> **ABRAHAM:** Two. Neither's good. You let them through — they reach the cities,
> casualties in the **billions.** Or—
> **PRESIDENT:** Or what?
> **ABRAHAM:** ...Or we put **Hellfire missiles** on them. UAV-launched. Each
> volley kills maybe a **quarter** of the swarm — and we fire again, and again.
> But sir — those drones don't just die. They come apart. **Burning shrapnel.
> Fireballs the size of cars, raining on whatever's underneath.** We save the
> many. We burn the few. Hundreds. Maybe thousands.
> **PRESIDENT:** *(a long, terrible silence)*
> **ABRAHAM:** Mr. President — do *not* give that order. There has to be another—
> **PRESIDENT:** There isn't. And you know it. *(beat)* Billions... or thousands.
> *(heavy)* ...God forgive me. Light them up.
> **ABRAHAM:** *(quiet, defeated)* ...Yes, Mr. President.

### HELLFIRE DEPLOYED

> *(Through the window of the CHINOOK Spike is flying, the sky ignites — Hellfire
> missiles streak in from the UAVs and tear into the swarm. Drones erupt by the
> dozens, whole sections of the formation vanishing in fire.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** *YES!* Look at 'em drop!
> **FOX:** That's it — knock 'em out of the sky!
> *(Everyone's cheering — everyone but ABRAHAM, who stares out the window, jaw
> tight, watching the fireballs begin their long fall.)*
> **ABRAHAM:** *(quietly)* ...It's not the sky I'm worried about.

### THE COST — RAIN OF FIRE

> *(EPIC MUSIC swells. CUT TO: the streets below. Civilians stop. Look up.
> **Hundreds of burning fireballs** — the wreckage of dead drones — come
> screaming down toward the city like a meteor shower. People scatter and run,
> dodging the fireballs as they slam through the streets like deadly fireworks.)*

### THE FALL

> *(Back to the Chinook. A burning drone SLAMS into the tail. Then another. Then
> another.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** We're hit — *WE'RE HIT—!*
> **FOX:** BRACE! BRACE FOR—
> *(The helicopter heels over and plummets into the city. Everyone braces. It
> STRIKES the ground — hard.)*
> *(BLACK. Three full seconds of silence.)*
> *(A CONCUSSION-GRENADE RING — and we punch into **SPIKE'S POV.** He's on his
> back in the wreckage, rubbing his head. Everything hurts. Muffled ringing. The
> world tilts back into focus.)*

### THROUGH THE BURNING CITY

> **SPIKE:** *(groaning)* ...Everybody up. *Up.* We can't stay here.
> *(The team drags itself out of the burning wreck, stumbling, dodging falling
> fireballs as they push deeper into the city toward **CITY HALL.**)*

### EXTRACT THE PRESIDENT

> *(They breach City Hall and reach the PRESIDENT.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** Mr. President — we're your way out. *Move!*
> *(His guards move to follow — and a cluster of burning drones CRASHES through
> the roof of the hall, wiping the guards out in an instant. Only the team and
> the President are left standing.)*
> **PRESIDENT:** *(shaken)* ...My men—
> **SPIKE:** No time. Stay on me!

### THE DRIVE OUT

> *(They hustle the President through back alleyways toward an escort vehicle.
> They find a **luxury car** and pile in — Spike at the wheel, tearing through the
> burning ashes of the city.)*
>
> *(A massive block of RUBBLE blocks the road. They bail and help the President
> over and through it on foot, fireballs still falling around them.)*

### ESCAPE

> *(Finally clear of the drones' kill-zone, a helicopter waits. They load the
> President aboard with the rest of the team and lift off — up and out of the
> inferno.)*
>
> *(— END OF MISSION 6. —)*

## NOTES / OPEN THREADS
- **The target:** the **annual state government dinner at the Texas town hall** —
  every state official in one room. HYDRA wants them all dead.
- **Drone behavior (key mechanic/lore):** H2.1 bombers **must expend their entire
  bomb payload before self-destructing** — which is *why* they carpet-bomb
  everything en route rather than flying straight to the target. Horrifying by
  design.
- **EMP Sharks — DEFINED:** unmanned 11ft×5ft remote-controlled mini-helicopters
  that open a rear latch and release several thousand EMP bomblets in a
  shotgun-blast spread, **~45-mile radius**, disabling everything electrically
  powered. Full spec: `game/design/emp-shark.md`. (Several are deployed here.)
- **The no-win choice (key dramatic beat):** EMP fails → President must choose
  **billions dead (let them pass)** vs **hundreds/thousands dead (Hellfire
  fireballs).** He picks Hellfire; **Abraham objects but is overruled.** A defining
  moral-weight moment — and a wedge between Abraham and command.
- **President survives** — the team extracts him from City Hall and escapes. His
  **guards are killed** by crashing drones; only Team Apex gets him out.
- **Full Team Apex deployed.** This mission has **every member deployed**,
  including the quieter operators **Brian Wolford, Jesse Wolford, Monroe Sydney**
  (see GAME_DESIGN roster). **The player is introduced to one of them here**
  (which — TBD). Good spot to give a background operator a memorable moment.
- **⚠ Era flag (minor, likely intentional):** **UAV-launched Hellfire missiles**
  didn't exist until ~2001 (Hellfire itself: 1984). In *this* alt-history world
  (HYDRA space rockets + drones since 1948), advanced armed UAVs in 1985 fit the
  established tech level. Keeping as intentional alt-history unless you say
  otherwise.
- **Why the EMP failed:** the H2.1s are electrical but **draw too much power** to
  be downed by even a full Shark blast — escalates the threat and forces the
  Hellfire choice.
- Sets up **Mission 7 "Industry"** (where the drones are built).
