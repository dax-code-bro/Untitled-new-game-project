## Setup / Objective
- Spike & Striker are sent to **retrieve intel from a major HYDRA base near
  Africa.**
- Intel reportedly available there on **two key targets:**
  1. **The person who recently used Drone 9** — i.e., Drone 9's *latest* employer
     (after Lady Death, who died in Mission 3). A fresh lead on the sentient drone.
  2. **A potential OWNER of HYDRA** — the first lead toward **who actually runs /
     owns HYDRA.** Major plot escalation — the hunt for the head of the snake.

## Gameplay Style
- **Very realistic tactical SWAT mission** — comparable to **Ready or Not.**
  Full SWAT gear, careful room clearing, hostage rescue, finishing-move takedowns,
  interrogation. A deliberate tonal change-up from the big action set-pieces
  (Hellfire) — grounded and methodical.

2. **Floor 1 — hostage rescue.** A hostage; **save them before they're shot**
   (fail state = hostage dies). Loot: **documents** (take them) and **stim shots**

   haven't seen a single one").

## REVEALS (canon)
- **Victor Prestige** — named as **HYDRA's leader** (the "owner of HYDRA" the op
  was hunting). First real name at the top of the org.
- **"Ray Abraham"** — someone **Victor Prestige has been talking to.** Surname
  **Abraham** — lands like a hammer given **General Abraham's** secrecy. See flag.
- **Drone intel is compartmentalized** — this base has **no drone intel** and its
  people have **never even seen a drone.** The drone program is walled off even
  within HYDRA. (So the **Drone 9 lead is a dead end here** — only the leadership
  names came out.)
