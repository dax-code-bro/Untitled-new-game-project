## DRAMATIC IRONY — ARMED
- The expedition walks onto the ice to kill Abraham — and **one of them is
  Abraham's**, twisted through the ghosts in his head (M18). Every scene is
  double-written: the team's plan, and what Spike might do to it. The player
  controls the compromised man.
- **Molotov's nightmare, unknowing:** he finally slept because the last threat
  was "a continent away." He's now traveling to it — **sitting next to it.**
- **The inversion of M11:** in 1985 the traitor (Abraham) *sent* the team into
  the ambush and stayed safe. In 2000 the traitor (Spike) **walks with them
  into the ice.** Whether that difference saves anyone is the endgame's
  question.
