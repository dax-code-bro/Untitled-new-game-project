# Mission 11 (title TBD) — The Abraham Betrayal / The Massacre

> **Canonical full script.** Preserves EVERY line and detail. Target rating:
> **ESRB M.** **⚠ Title TBD** (needs a foreshadowing title per the naming
> convention). Number tentative (follows Mission 10).

## The big turn
**General Abraham is the traitor.** He uses Team Apex to retrieve the final piece
of intel, then leads them into an ambush and **annihilates the team.** The
"Ray Abraham" thread (Mission 9) and Spike's confirmation (Mission 10) pay off
here in blood.

## POV / Setup
- **POV: Striker**, with **Payback and Fox** lined up beside him, on a **hill
  overlooking a huge outpost and a forest.**
- **Overwatch:** the **Wolford twins (Brian & Jesse)** + **four other operators**
  position on the hill to cover/support the trio. *(At least two of the "four"
  are Abraham's planted men — see the end.)*

## Briefing (Abraham, over radio)
> **ABRAHAM:** *(radio)* Take down the Hydra leader — and we don't just bloody
> Hydra's nose, we **dismantle all of it.** We've got major intel on him. There's
> just **one more piece** we need before we can pin his exact location. Go get it.

## Gameplay — The Outpost
- The **trio descends the cliff** into the outpost; lock and load.
- **63 enemies** to clear — including **snipers** and **bazooka troops** (take
  those out quickly) — fighting toward the **inner circle.**

### BOSS — HYDRA Juggernaut
- HYDRA drops in a **heavy/juggernaut**: **minigun + insane armor.**
- **Three stages**, each with roughly the **health of three armored HYDRA
  soldiers:**
  1. **Stage 1 →** his **helmet breaks off** (now **headshots do double damage**).
  2. **Stage 2 →** he **loses the minigun**, switches to an **assault rifle**.
  3. **Stage 3 (final) →** defeated, he **dies.**

### Push to the middle
- Reach a **scientist outpost** — the trio **retrieve the documents** (the "one
  more piece").
- Continue to a **small clearing** where Abraham says the **leader should be.**

## THE BETRAYAL (the clearing)

> *(The clearing is fortified: **tanks, sandbags, Humvees, large vehicles**, with
> **soldiers positioned behind the sandbags.** ABRAHAM is here. Striker walks up
> and hands him the documents.)*
>
> **ABRAHAM:** *(almost gently)* ...I mean — next time, just **don't try to
> double-check.** That's all you had to do.
>
> *(He backs away. The trio exchange a confused look.)*
>
> *(The soldiers behind the sandbags rise — their weapons revealed as **LMGs** —
> and open fire. **500+ rounds of 5.56×45mm NATO** tear into the trio from every
> angle. There is no surviving it.)*

## FOX'S DEATH

> *(POV: **FOX**, hit, dragging himself across the dirt — trying to crawl away.)*
>
> *(ABRAHAM walks up, calm, unhurried. He draws his **golden Colt M1911.**)*
>
> *(He levels it at Fox's head — and fires.)*

## THE TWINS

> *(Sniper scope. **BRIAN and JESSE**, watching it all through their glass.)*
>
> **JESSE:** ...no. No, no — that son of a bitch. **Abraham double-crossed us!**
> **BRIAN:** *(swinging his crosshair onto Abraham's head)* Got you—
>
> *(TWO GUNSHOTS.)*
>
> *(The camera pulls back: **two operators** stand behind the twins, a **revolver
> smoking** from the barrel. **Brian and Jesse slump dead** over their rifles.
> The two operators **holster their guns and walk away — down the hill**, the same
> way out.)*

> *(— END OF MISSION 11. —)*

- **Fox — KIA.** Executed by Abraham (golden Colt M1911, to the head).
- **Brian Wolford — KIA.** Shot by Abraham's planted operators.
- **Jesse Wolford — KIA.** Shot by Abraham's planted operators.
- **Payback — KIA.** Cut down in the LMG ambush — **500+ rounds of 5.56×45mm
  NATO** across her whole body. Definitively dead; no survival.
- **Striker — KIA.** Same — shredded by the LMG ambush (500+ rounds of 5.56).
  Definitively dead.
- **The four overwatch operators:** at least two were Abraham's plants (the
  killers); the others' fate TBD.

**Team Apex is effectively annihilated.** Known survivors: **Spike** (crippled,
Mission 10) and **Molotov** (in hiding). The heroes are reduced to a paralyzed
man and a man who already walked away — against a HYDRA now backed by their own
general.
