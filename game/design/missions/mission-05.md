# Mission 05 — "A Lost Cause"

> **Canonical script (in progress).** Preserves EVERY line of dialogue and EVERY

**Title:** "A Lost Cause" *(LOCKED.)*

## Placement
- Set up by **Mission 4's 3-day cruise-ship voyage.** Spike & Molotov are at sea,
  heading to a destination (TBD), having escaped the island.
- **Sets up Mission 6, "Hellfire."**

## Title Meaning (foreshadow — RESOLVED)
"A Lost Cause" = **what the team called Spike and Molotov when they thought they
were dead.** When command heard the exfil failed — no boat, no signal, six days
of silence — they **wrote the two of them off as a lost cause**, presumed
"gone below." The title is the team's grief for men who turned out to be alive.
Abraham delivers the payoff at HQ.

## BEAT SHEET (opening — every detail, in order)
1. **End of the voyage.** Picks up at the **end of the 3-day voyage** Spike &
   Molotov survived; they're **arriving at a dock** (friendly territory, near
   their lines).

## FULL SCRIPT

### ARRIVAL — END OF THE VOYAGE

> *(Open water giving way to a coastline. After three days at sea, the cruise
> ship drifts toward a DOCK. Spike at the helm; Molotov beside him, head still
> bandaged but steadier than the island.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** ...Land. Actual land.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(dry)* Don't get sentimental.

### THE SIGNAL

> *(As they reach the dock, Spike keys his radio — reaching for the team for the
> first time in days.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** This is Spike. Anyone on this channel — *this is Spike,* do you copy?
> *(A burst of static — then the channel erupts.)*
> **TEAM (radio):** ...Spike?! *(overlapping voices)* — he's alive — they're
> *ALIVE* —
> **FOX (radio):** Spike, where in God's name have you— *(catches himself)* —
> never mind. Stay put. We're sending people immediately. There's an outpost
> twenty minutes from you, chopper's already spinning up. Do *not* move.
> **SPIKE:** ...Copy. We're not going anywhere.

### THE PICKUP

> *(They barely have time to breathe — seconds — before a HELICOPTER thunders in
> over the treeline and flares onto the dock. The door slides open.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** ...That was fast.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(eyeing it)* ...Twenty minutes, my ass.
>
> *(They climb aboard. The chopper lifts off and banks toward HQ.)*

### THE RIDE BACK

> *(Inside, the relief is real — they made it. As the helicopter carries them
> home, the math settles in: the mansion, the island, three days at sea — all
> told, they were gone **almost six days.** Six days written off as dead.)*
>
### HQ — "A LOST CAUSE" (title payoff)

> *(HQ. Spike and Molotov get situated — cleaned up, Molotov's head finally
> dressed properly. GENERAL ABRAHAM finds them. For once, the hard man looks
> shaken.)*
>
> **ABRAHAM:** When the exfil went dark... when we heard it failed... *(a long
> breath)* We thought you were both **a lost cause.** No boat. No signal. Six
> days of silence. *(quietly)* We thought you'd gone below.
> **SPIKE:** ...We almost did. *(beat)* We did, for a while.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(touching the bandage)* ...Some of us more than others.
>
> *(— CONTINUES. —)*
