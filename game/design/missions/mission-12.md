# Mission 12 (title TBD) — Molotov Finds the Massacre

## POV
- **Molotov** (Webber Newman). His grief sequence — the aftermath of the Mission
  11 massacre, discovered too late.

## Setup
- After failing to save Spike (Mission 10), Molotov's next resolve: if he can't
  save Spike, he'll **warn the rest of the team that Abraham is a rat** (traitor).
- He races to do it — and arrives to find the team already dead.

## FULL SCRIPT

### THE EMPTY HQ

> *(Molotov heads to the HQ. He unlocks the door — and finds it **completely
> empty.** Dead silence.)*
>
> *(He climbs the stairs to the **mission room.** Clues are scattered everywhere.
> On the wall: **info about the team's latest mission** — the op they deployed on.
> He studies it. Then he moves.)*

### THE TRAIL

> *(Molotov gets in his car and drives to the location. As he nears, he sees
> them: **several — maybe hundreds — of HYDRA AND American military vehicles**
> rolling out of the clearing. He follows their trail.)*

### THE CLIFF — THE TWINS

> *(On the cliff, he finds the bodies of the **WOLFORD TWINS — Brian and Jesse.**
> He drops beside them, quickly checks for life. Nothing. **No time to mourn.**)*
>
> *(He takes **Brian's sniper**, brings it to his eye, and scopes the clearing
> below — **three dead soldiers.**)*

### THE CLEARING — FOX

> *(He runs down off the cliff, right up beside the bodies. He sees the face—)*
>
> *(—**Fox.**)*
>
> *(Molotov collapses onto one knee. Then the other. Shaking. He looks down at
> both his hands. Through the **broken plate of his half-shattered mask** — the
> mask that broke against Spike — a **single tear** forms, and falls down his
> face.)*
>
> *(He gathers Fox's body against him, and whispers one word.)*
>
> **MOLOTOV:** ...I'm sorry.
>
> *(— END OF MISSION 12. —)*

## WHY IT LANDS / THREADS
- **The stoic breaks — and ONLY for Fox.** This is the key. Molotov held his
  **exact same serious, flat tone** the entire Spike sequence (M10) — he didn't
  soften when he *agreed* with Spike, and he **didn't cry or waver after he shot
  and crippled him.** Iron composure through all of it. So the fact that **Fox's
  body is what finally shatters him** — one tear, collapse to both knees,
  "I'm sorry" — tells us, with zero exposition, that **Fox meant more to him than
  anyone.** The unbreakable man had exactly one breaking point, and it was Fox.
  (Fox took him in, founded the team that became his family, called him "Molly.")
- **His arc of failure:** couldn't save his little brother (flashback), couldn't
  save Spike (M10), now couldn't save the team. **"I'm sorry"** is the weight of
  all of it. This guilt drives his post-campaign path (the island, "we pay our
  debts," finally leaving).
- **⚠ American vehicles alongside HYDRA (big):** the ambush used **US military
  assets *with* HYDRA** — Abraham's betrayal reaches **into the American
  military/government.** The corruption goes higher than one general. Major
  conspiracy thread.
- **Confirms Molotov's whereabouts** during the massacre — trying (and failing)
  to warn them. Fills the gap between M10 and his post-campaign survival.
- **Mask note:** still half-broken from the Spike fight (M10) — the tear falls
  through the gap. Nice visual continuity.
