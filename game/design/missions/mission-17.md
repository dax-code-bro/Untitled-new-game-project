## THE STORMING (full scene)

> *(Dawn, or something the ruined sky calls dawn. Prestige's base squats behind
> its walls. The team stacks up with **Hunter's militia — dozens of them.**)*
>
> **DICE:** *(eyeing the militia's prep)* ...what are they doing with the rope?
> **HUNTER:** *(pleasant)* Improvising.
>
> *(Hunter's men **bundle grenades and tanks together with rope** — crude
> cluster-bombs, fuel and frag lashed into single catastrophic packages. They
> **ignite them** and **hurl them at the base.**)*
>
> *(The wall of the compound simply **ceases to be a wall.** Catastrophic,
> instant — a rolling concussion that knocks dust off the whole valley.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** *(ears ringing)* ...subtle.
> **HUNTER:** I don't do subtle. I do *finished.*
>
> *(They rush the breach. Inside is an inferno — **engineers and soldiers burn
> alive**, stumbling out of the smoke, some already gone, some still fighting.
> The group pushes forward through it toward the **garage.**)*
>
> *(A **Humvee comes screaming out of the fire — fully ablaze, a man burning
> alive at the wheel, screaming** — dead man's foot on the gas. It rockets
> straight at the group—)*
>
> **ATOMIC:** *MOVE—!*
>
> *(They barely dive clear. The burning Humvee misses by a breath, **slams into
> a rock, and detonates instantly** — a second fireball folding into the
> first.)*
>
> *(They reach the **garage.** Everything — *everything* — is on fire.)*
>
> *(— CONTINUES. —)*

## SCENE NOTES / FLAGS
- **Hunter's siege doctrine:** **rope-bundled grenades + fuel tanks, ignited and
  thrown** — improvised cluster incendiaries. Catastrophic, indiscriminate,
  theatrical. Exactly his "cruel but fair / I do finished" personality as
  *tactics.*
- **Tone:** the assault is a horror-tinged inferno — burning engineers, a
  screaming immolated driver, a base eaten by fire. Full escalated-M intensity.
- **⚠ Irony worth noting (not yet voiced):** MOLOTOV — the man of fire, the man
  who *hates* what fire did to him — is storming a base that is entirely on
  fire, alongside an ally who fights with fire. Whether the scene leans into
  that (a look, a beat, his one-and-only reaction) is the creator's call. Big
  potential moment; not assumed.
- **⚠ Where's Prestige?** The garage is the objective now — presumably his exit
  route (the lab was underground; the garage = escape). Confirm.
- **Hunter's bullet** (drone-shrapnel round for Prestige) is in play this
  mission.
