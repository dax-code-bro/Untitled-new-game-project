# Mission 01 — "█████████" (Objective: CLASSIFIED)

> **Canonical full script.** This file preserves EVERY line of dialogue and
> EVERY story detail for Mission 1, start to finish. Nothing here is to be lost
> or summarized away. Working title only; the in-game mission name is redacted
> on purpose (see Cold Open).
>
> Target rating: **ESRB M** — intense violence, strong language, dark humor.
> Setting recorded as **"Columbia, USA — 1985"** per original direction.
> ⚠ Open flag: the landmark **Torre del Reloj** is real to **Cartagena,
> Colombia** — Colombia-vs-Columbia decision still pending (see GAME_DESIGN.md).

**Cast in this mission:**
- **Spike** — Rodriguez "Spike" Hamilton, the player character.
- **Fox** — James "Fox" Carter, 65, Irish, squad founder & leader. (Benelli M3 Super 90.)
- **Molotov** — Webber "Molotov" Newman, silent masked all-rounder. (M16 + flame charm.)
- **Striker** — John "Striker" Jones, ~24, the rookie. (PP919 SMG.) Fox calls him "Mr. 24."
- **Payback** — nameless operator, scavenger, uses whatever's available.
- **Abraham** — General Abraham, four-star general. (Gold Colt M1911.) Appears at the regroup.

---

## BEAT SHEET (every detail, in order)

1. **Cold open** — helicopter inbound; redacted radio; title cards: location/date
   + OBJECTIVE: CLASSIFIED + MISSION: CLASSIFIED.
2. **Fly-over** — the squad flies over the city; below is a huge pile of smoke
   and despair. The chopper heads straight toward it.
3. **Rooftop LZ** — the helicopter lands in a clearing on top of a building.
4. **Gear up (control hand-off)** — inside the chopper, Spike picks up his rifle
   and magazines. Player takes control.
5. **Deploy** — the squad disembarks together.
6. **The descent** — down a staircase, slowly deeper into the building.
7. **F-16 crash** — exiting the door, an F-16 crashes right beside Spike and the
   team; Molotov and the others move to cover.
8. **First wave (tutorial fight)** — ~16 low-accuracy enemies; one M16 burst
   drops each; teammates assist.
9. **The clearing** — push toward the main area; reach a safe pocket enemies
   can't reach. Fox gives orders.
10. **Squad split** — Fox + Molotov ("Molly"); Striker ("Mr. 24") + Payback
    escort Spike toward a watchtower sniper at Torre del Reloj.
11. **City push** — fight through, wave after wave, everyone under fire, until
    reaching **Torre del Reloj (the Clock Tower)**.
12. **Clock tower** — climb; stealth-kill two guards sniping the team; pick up
    and reload their sniper; scope the city; spot **3 friendly US Army tanks**,
    then **several black HYDRA tanks** with the three-headed-serpent crest.
13. **Anti-tank stand** — the captured rifle holds anti-tank rounds; destroy
    **3 tanks (3 shots each)** before they breach the main line and overrun your men.
14. **Regroup** — descend to the battlefield; Abraham (on the field) shakes
    Spike's hand; the rattled squad demands answers about HYDRA; Abraham shuts
    it down: "That's none of your concern. For the current moment." Move out.

**Threads planted:** Abraham knows HYDRA and withholds it. Payback recognizes
HYDRA and clocks Abraham's secret. Molotov breaks silence just to name them.

## FULL SCRIPT

### 1. COLD OPEN

> *(FADE IN — BLACK. The slow, building THUD of rotor blades. A radio cuts
> through static, clipped and official — words drop out, redacted by the
> mission itself.)*
>
> **RADIO (V.O.):** ...██████ is green. Bird is wheels-up. Package is —
> ████████ —
> *(static swallows it)*
> **RADIO (V.O.):** Remember. You were never here.
>
> *(Title cards stamp hard over black:)*
>
>     COLUMBIA, USA — 1985
>     OBJECTIVE: [ CLASSIFIED ]
>     MISSION:   [ CLASSIFIED ]

### 2. FLY-OVER

> *(CUT TO: the helicopter, low over the city. Below — a vast pile of smoke and
> despair. Whole blocks burning. The bird banks and heads straight for the worst
> of it.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** *(at the open door, staring down)* ...Madre de Dios. Is the
> *whole city* on fire?
> **FOX:** *(thumbing shells into the Benelli, never looking up)* Most of it.
> The rest is working on it.
> **STRIKER:** How are you so *calm?*
> **FOX:** Forty years of practice, Mr. 24. Strap in.
>
> *(MOLOTOV checks his M16 in silence, the little flame charm swinging from the
> rail. Striker watches him.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** ...Does he ever talk?
> **PAYBACK:** When it's worth saying.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(deadpan, not looking up)* You're in my seat.
> *(Striker scrambles over a bench. Under the mask, you can almost hear Molotov
> smile.)*

### 3. ROOFTOP LZ

> **PILOT (V.O.):** LZ's hot — rooftop's the only clear pad! Sixty seconds!
> **FOX:** You heard the man. Spike — gear up.

### 4. GEAR UP (control hand-off)

> **FOX:** Rifle's yours, son. Mags are on the rack — take what you can carry.
>
> *(PLAYER CONTROL BEGINS. Spike picks up his rifle and magazines.)*
>
> **PILOT (V.O.):** Touchdown! Go, go, go!

### 5. DEPLOY

> **FOX:** Off the bird! On me!
> *(The squad disembarks onto the rooftop together.)*

### 6. THE DESCENT

> **FOX:** Stairwell's our way in. We go quiet, we go deep. Eyes open.
> **STRIKER:** *(under his breath)* Quiet. Right. Quiet.
>
> *(Descending the staircase. Muffled gunfire and screams bleed up through the
> walls.)*
>
> **PAYBACK:** *(low)* Contact below us. The whole city's a meat grinder.

### 7. F-16 CRASH

> *(Spike shoulders the door open — smoke and grey daylight. Then a SCREAM of a
> dying engine overhead—)*
>
> **FOX:** INCOMING — *DOWN!*
>
> *(An F-16 spirals out of the smoke and SLAMS into the street feet from the
> squad. A wall of fire and shrapnel. Molotov and the others dive to cover.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** *(coughing)* Was that one of *OURS?!*
> **FOX:** Does it *matter?* COVER!

### 8. FIRST WAVE

> **PAYBACK:** Contacts, front — sixteen, maybe more!
> **FOX:** Green militia — they couldn't hit water from a boat! Short bursts —
> drop 'em clean!
>
> *(Combat. ~16 enemies, deliberately poor accuracy; one M16 burst kills each.
> Teammates lay in supporting fire.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** *(firing)* They're not even *aiming!*
> **FOX:** Don't complain about a gift, lad — keep firing!
>
> *(As the last enemy drops:)*
>
> **FOX:** That's the lot. Push up — toward the main square.

### 9. THE CLEARING & 10. SQUAD SPLIT

> *(They advance through rubble and bodies, clearing wave after wave, until they
> reach a sheltered pocket — defilade, no angle in.)*
>
> **FOX:** Hold here. They can't reach us in this. Catch your breath.
> *(Beat. Fox lays it out.)*
> **FOX:** Right. Molly — you're with me.
> *(MOLOTOV nods.)*
> **FOX:** Mr. 24 — stay attentive. You and Miss Payback are gonna clear the way
> for Spike.
> **FOX:** There's a sniper in a watchtower, not far. Torre del Reloj — the old
> clock tower. He's picking my men apart one by one. Put him down and take his
> perch.
> **PAYBACK:** On it.
> **FOX:** Spike — you're the trigger. They'll get you there. Don't waste the climb.

### 11. CITY PUSH → 12. THE CLOCK TOWER

> *(Spike, Striker, and Payback fight through the streets under constant fire —
> wave after wave — until they reach the base of Torre del Reloj. A staircase
> winds up into the dark.)*
>
> **PAYBACK:** *(low)* Two up top. Snipers — they've got eyes on Fox's element.
> **STRIKER:** *(whisper)* How do you wanna—
> **PAYBACK:** Quiet. Blades. Spike, take the left. I've got the right.
>
> *(Player climbs and stealth-kills the first guard; Payback takes the second.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** *(radio, hushed)* Mr. 24's got the stairs. Go.
> **PAYBACK:** Clear. Grab the rifle — you'll want the glass.
>
> *(Player reloads the dropped sniper, aims down sights, zooms over the city.)*
>
> **FOX:** *(radio)* Spike, talk to me. What do you see?
> **SPIKE:** ...Armor. Three tanks, rolling in from the north.
> **STRIKER:** Those ours?
> **SPIKE:** US Army markings. Cavalry's here.
> **FOX:** *(radio)* About bloody time.
>
> *(Beat. More tanks roll in at the far end — black, unmarked. The scope tightens
> on the hull: a three-headed serpent.)*
>
> **SPIKE:** ...Wait.
> **STRIKER:** What? What is it?
> **SPIKE:** More armor. But they're black. No US markings.
> **PAYBACK:** *(cold)* Zoom in. The hull.
> **SPIKE:** There's a symbol. Some kind of snake. Three heads.
> **FOX:** *(radio, long pause)* ...Say that again.
> **PAYBACK:** *(quiet)* Hydra.
> **STRIKER:** Who the hell are Hydra?
> **FOX:** *(radio, grim)* Trouble we were told didn't exist. Everyone — off the
> X. Now.

### 13. ANTI-TANK STAND

> *(Spike still holds the captured rifle. Below, the black HYDRA tanks grind
> toward the main line, tracer fire lighting the smoke.)*
>
> **PAYBACK:** *(grabs the breech, racks a fat round)* Wait — this isn't a
> marksman rig. It's **anti-tank**. They came up here to crack armor.
> **STRIKER:** Then what's it doing pointed at *people?*
> **PAYBACK:** Doesn't matter what it *was* for. Spike — it matters now.
> **FOX:** *(radio, gunfire behind him)* Spike! Those tanks reach the line and
> we're done — the whole element, gone! You've got the high seat — *use it!*
>
> **SPIKE:** *(settling the scope)* Loading. Talk me on.
> **PAYBACK:** Treads first — slow 'em down. Then the turret ring. Breathe
> between shots.
>
> *(— FIRST TANK —)*
> **SPIKE:** First one's lined up.
> *(THOOM.)*
> **PAYBACK:** Tread's gone. He's blind and crawling. Finish him.
> *(THOOM. THOOM.)*
> **STRIKER:** *(whoops)* That's a kill — he's brewing up!
> **FOX:** *(radio)* One down — two to go, and they are *not* slowing!
>
> *(— SECOND TANK —)*
> **SPIKE:** Second's turning on Fox's position.
> **FOX:** *(radio)* I'm *well* aware! Less talking, more shooting, lad!
> *(THOOM. THOOM. THOOM.)*
> **PAYBACK:** Splash. Cooked.
>
> *(— THIRD TANK, closest to the line —)*
> **STRIKER:** Last one's on the barricade — they're gonna breach!
> **SPIKE:** Not today.
> *(THOOM. THOOM.)*
> **PAYBACK:** One more — center mass!
> *(THOOM. The third tank erupts. A beat — then cheering from the line below.)*
> **FOX:** *(radio, ragged)* ...Line holds. Bloody beautiful shooting, Spike.
> Get down here.
> **PAYBACK:** *(sets the rifle down, quiet)* ...They sent armor. For us.
> *(beat)* That's not a warning. That's a hunt.

### 14. REGROUP

> *(Spike descends into the smoke and wreckage. ABRAHAM is on the battlefield;
> he strides over and extends a hand.)*
>
> **ABRAHAM:** Good shooting, son. *(firm handshake)* You just bought us the line.
>
> *(The squad gathers — battered, rattled. Several look furious.)*
>
> **STRIKER:** Sir — with respect — what the hell was that? Those tanks. That
> symbol.
> **MOLOTOV:** *(rare, low — he almost never speaks)* Hydra.
> **STRIKER:** Who *are* Hydra? Why do they have armor? Why does nobody—
> **FOX:** *(stepping in)* Easy, Mr. 24.
> **PAYBACK:** *(quiet, eyes locked on Abraham)* ...He knows.
>
> *(All eyes turn to Abraham.)*
>
> **ABRAHAM:** *(flat)* That's none of your concern. For the current moment.
> **STRIKER:** None of our—? They almost wiped us out!
> **ABRAHAM:** And you're still breathing. Keep it that way. Move out.
>
> *(— END OF MISSION 1. —)*

## CONTINUITY NOTES (do not forget)

- Fox's nicknames: **Molotov = "Molly," Striker = "Mr. 24"** (he's 24).
- **Molotov almost never talks** — every spoken line from him is a deliberate,
  weighty moment.
- **Payback knows HYDRA on sight** and is the first to name them; she also reads
  that Abraham is hiding something. Her arc is tied to HYDRA.
- **Abraham is withholding HYDRA intel** from his own people — central mystery.
- The captured tower sniper is an **anti-materiel (anti-tank) rifle** — that's
  why it can kill tanks.
- Fail state of the anti-tank stand: tanks breach the main line → your men are
  overrun.
- **Open setting flag:** Columbia (USA) vs Colombia (country) — unresolved.
