# Dragon's Kingdom: dragon designs

This file holds Daxtyn's own decisions about every dragon. Anything written here **overrides** the screenplay handoffs, the asset bible (`episodes/s01e01/assets.json` / `assets.md`) and any provisional design in the code. Anything not written here is still provisional.

## Creator changes to canon

**No dragon species (decided 2026-10-09).** Daxtyn scrapped the species *Bashion*, *Nightwing* and *Slitherwing*. Every dragon is its own custom design. Dragons no longer share a species body. Leaf and Starlight are not "the same species", and the gold hatchling and the scout are their own designs too.

- **Species names in the dialogue: decided 2026-10-18 (question closed).** The Episode 1 dialogue used to name the scrapped species. Daxtyn chose the suggested wording ("use the suggestions"):
  - Abby's [ORIGINAL] line "Why couldn't I get a Bashion?" becomes "Why couldn't I get one like Charcoal?". This is Daxtyn's own line, and he approved the change.
  - Remi's "Nightwings are faster. More versatile." and Abby's "And Bashions?" (L053, L054) become "Smaller ones are faster. More versatile." and "And big ones?". Remi's answer "Better for war." (L055) stays.
  - Remi's "We were off the coast. Flying together. Something came past Leaf. A Slitherwing." (L109) becomes "...Something came past Leaf. A small dragon. Fast."
  - Abby's "I still want a Bashion." (addition X17) becomes "I still want one like Charcoal."

  No Episode 1 line names a species any more. The old wording is kept for the record in the shooting script's WAS text (`episodes/s01e01/final/screenplay-final.md`, changes C8b, C9, C19 and C30) and in `episodes/s01e01/final/CHANGES.md` section 9. The handoff `episodes/s01e01/screenplay.md` itself is never edited.

**Every dragon has two legs and horns (decided 2026-10-14).** All dragons have **only two legs**, not four, and **every dragon has horns**. The wings are the front limbs, like a bat's or a pterosaur's. On the ground a dragon stands on its two hind legs and can lean on its folded wings (the wrist joint) when it walks, as bats and pterosaurs do. There are no separate front legs.

**Low, normal stance (decided 2026-10-16).** Daxtyn wants the dragons to look more normal: **low to the ground, with the two legs placed more toward the center of the body** (under the middle of the body, not at the back). The body stays roughly level and low, like a big animal crouching, with the folded wings' wrists touching down in front for support when walking or resting. They do not stand tall and upright like a rearing horse.

**Bodies designed by Claude (decided 2026-10-14).** Daxtyn gives the colours and key facts. He asked Claude to design each dragon's actual body to look as realistic as possible, based on how real animals are built. Those body designs are shown to Daxtyn as pictures, and he can change anything.

**Leaf's size (decided 2026-10-09).** Leaf is about **one quarter (25%) of Charcoal's size**, not tiny. This replaces the screenplay's "about one-twentieth Charcoal's size or smaller". Working reading: Leaf's body length is about 25% of Charcoal's.

## Charcoal (Remi's dragon)

Approved by Daxtyn on 2026-10-09:

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Scales | **Completely black** |
| Eyes | **Blue** |
| Wing membrane | **Gray with black stripes throughout** |
| Fire | **Standard orange-red fire** |

From the screenplay (still true): immense, generally calm, slow to anger. His takeoff devastates the ground.

Body (Claude designs it; Daxtyn reviews pictures): two legs, wings as front limbs, horns. Size about 36 m long. The black stripes run along the wing's finger bones.

## Leaf (Abby's dragon)

Approved by Daxtyn on 2026-10-09:

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Size | About **25% of Charcoal's size** |
| Scales | **Dark green** |
| Eyes | **Yellow** |
| Wing membrane | **Light green** |
| Fire | A unique **purplish-blue fire**. It burns **hotter than normal dragon fire, but not by a lot**. |

From the screenplay (still true): not fully grown. Leaf does not breathe fire on screen in Episode 1.

Body (Claude designs it; Daxtyn reviews pictures): two legs, wings as front limbs, horns. Still open: whether Leaf sits upright like a dog (with two legs, he sits back on his haunches with his wings folded).
