# Dragon's Kingdom: characters

This file holds Daxtyn's own decisions about the people of the series, next to the few facts the Episode 1 screenplay (`episodes/s01e01/screenplay.md`, its CANON LOCK) states about them. Daxtyn's decisions here override the screenplay handoffs, the episode plans and every provisional design. Anything not written here is still provisional: faces, costumes, voices, exact ages and every detail the episode additions propose (`SERIES.md` section 5). The dragons are in `DRAGONS.md`.

Each fact says where it comes from:
- **Daxtyn** means his own decision, with its date.
- **Screenplay** means the Episode 1 handoff says so.

## Adults and children (for the content limits)

The show is rated for mature audiences (`SERIES.md` section 8). Its limits for children apply to every character who is a child or could be one: no suggestive material, and no drugs, tobacco or alcohol, ever involves them.

| Character | Adult or child | Source | Content limits for children |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prince Remi IV (Remi) | **Adult** | Daxtyn, 2026-10-18: "Remi is an ADULT" (he may drink, swear and so on) | Do not apply |
| Abby | **A child** | Screenplay: scene 2A calls her "the injured child" | Apply |
| Queen Alexandria | Adult | Screenplay: the queen of Verdor and the mother of Remi and Abby | Do not apply |
| Queen Fall of Tara | Adult | Screenplay: a reigning queen and Starlight's rider | Do not apply |
| The King of Cling | Adult | Screenplay: the king of Cling, which lies within Scrapper | Do not apply |
| The child in Cling (CHILD) | **A child** | Screenplay | Apply |

The handoff says not to invent anyone's exact age (CANON LOCK item 2), so no exact age is set for anyone. Being an adult or a child is all that is decided.

The current Episode 1 shooting script also has proposed characters whose ages are not set, such as the young attendant in the palace and the young riders at the riding school. They are treated as if they could be children, so the limits apply to them too (`SERIES.md` section 8: "characters who could be children").

## Remi (Prince Remi IV)

- **Daxtyn, 2026-10-18:** Remi is an adult. He may drink, swear and so on. His exact age is not set.
- **Screenplay:** Prince Remi IV of Verdor, Abby's older brother. Queen Alexandria is his mother. He rides Charcoal. In flight he tells Abby she will be queen when he is gone.
- **In the Episode 1 shooting script:** he swears once (a proposed rating-pass line). Nothing in the script has him drink, smoke or take anything yet; Daxtyn said he may, and any such beat would be a new proposal.

## Abby

- **Screenplay:** Remi's younger sister, rider of Leaf. Queen Alexandria is her mother. The scout's pass breaks her LEFT arm in Episode 1. She never answers the succession question. Scene 2A calls her "the injured child", so the content limits for children apply to her (`SERIES.md` section 8). Her broken arm is never shown as a graphic bone image.
- Her exact age is not set.

## Queen Alexandria

- **Screenplay:** the queen of Verdor, mother of Remi and Abby. Her exact line at the hatching is "My pretty little weapon" ([ORIGINAL]). She orders the Santa Maria to bring back the fallen rider and dragon. She is not Queen Fall.
- An adult, because the screenplay makes her the reigning queen and the mother of Remi.

## Queen Fall of Tara

- **Screenplay:** Starlight's rider. She is not Queen Alexandria, and King Fallen of Scrapper is another person again. In Episode 1 she says only "Attack."
- An adult, because the screenplay makes her a reigning queen.

## The King of Cling

- **Screenplay:** the king of Cling, introduced at the festival in scene 3A. His personal name is not given, and none may be invented.
- An adult, because the screenplay makes him the reigning king.

## Everyone else

Still provisional. That includes every supporting role of the handoff and of the Episode 1 additions and rating pass (the keepers, the healer, the ship's master, the sailor, the vendor, the musicians, the farm family, the old woman, the watchman, the guards and others), their looks and their voices. They become canon only when Daxtyn accepts them (`SERIES.md` section 5). In the shooting script the farm family's two small children are children; every one of the dead in Cling is an adult (Daxtyn approved keeping those deaths on 2026-10-18).
