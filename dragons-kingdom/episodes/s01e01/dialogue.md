# Dragon's Kingdom, Season 1 Episode 1: Dialogue

This is the recording script: who says what, scene by scene. It is made from `screenplay.md` by `tools/extract_dialogue.py`, so it always matches the screenplay. **No voices have been recorded and nothing has been rendered yet.**

## How to read it

- Only the words in the **Line** column are spoken. The **How** column is advice for the performer; never say it out loud.
- **ORIGINAL** marks Daxtyn's own four lines. Say them exactly as written, word for word. (The word "ORIGINAL" is never spoken or shown.)
- A dash at the end of a line (—) means the speaker gets cut off.
- **CMD** marks a dragon command. Attack = physical attack. Fire = flame. Hold = stop. Skit (retreat) and Free fire are not used in this episode. "Back", "Again", "Home. Come on." and "Leaf. Here." are ordinary directions to a dragon, not new official commands.
- Dragons never speak.
- *Sec* is a rough guess at how long the line takes to say. Real recordings will be different.
