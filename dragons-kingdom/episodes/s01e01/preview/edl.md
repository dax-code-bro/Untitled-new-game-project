## Conventions

- **Frames.** Global frame 0 is the first frame of the WORK IN PROGRESS card. `start` is inclusive and `end` is exclusive, so each shot's end is the next shot's start. Timecode is HH:MM:SS:FF at 24 fps.
- **Line in-points.** "take @ frame" is the frame where the WAV's first sample goes; at 48 kHz one frame is 2000 samples. "speech" gives the frames where the words start and end, taken from the sentence segments in `takes.json`.
- **Cue gaps.** The gap runs from the end of one line's speech to the first sound of the next take. Quick exchanges use about 0.3 s, normal ones 0.35 to 0.4 s, and airborne speakers 0.45 to 0.5 s, so there is wind between the lines.
- **Shot length.** A shot is held at the shot-list estimate. The real takes are about 16 % shorter than estimated, so the spare time goes to the action and reactions after the lines. Pauses between lines are not stretched, and shots are not squeezed.
- **Dissolves.** Each dissolve is centred on the cut. Both shots render half the dissolve as a handle.
- **Camera.** Lenses are in mm on a Super 35 sensor, the same as the style frames.
- **Staging.** Faces and lip-sync are unfinished, so every line is staged with the speaker's mouth unreadable. The tools are: from behind (BACK), wide (WIDE), silhouette or contre-jour (SIL), hands (HANDS), shallow focus (DOF), over the shoulder (OTS) and off screen (OFF). What happens in the story does not change.
- **Faces deferred.** Some beats are played on a face in the screenplay: 1A-14, 1A-15, 1D-07, 1D-25, 1E-17. Here posture, breath and light carry them. They go back to faces once faces are approved.
