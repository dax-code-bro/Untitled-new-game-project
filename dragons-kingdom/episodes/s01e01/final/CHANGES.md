## 5. How to cut or undo something

All of this is in `screenplay-final.md`:

- **To cut an addition,** delete everything from its `[ADDITION X# ... — begin]` line to its `[ADDITION X# — end]` line. Any rating-pass piece inside it goes with it.
- **To cut a rating-pass piece,** delete everything from `[RATING PASS R# — begin]` to `[RATING PASS R# — end]`.
- **To undo a changed line,** replace the whole `[CHANGED C# ...]` block with the lines under its "WAS" marker.
- **Then run** `python3 tools/check_final.py --update-timing --clean` (from `episodes/s01e01/`), and after that `python3 tools/check_final.py` with no options. The tool tells you if something else leaned on what you cut. For example, R7, R8, C20, C23, C24 and C25 all lean on C15 (the scout's blood on Charcoal and Remi).
