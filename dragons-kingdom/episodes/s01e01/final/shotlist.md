# Dragon's Kingdom, Episode 1: shot list of the locked shooting script

Every shot of the whole episode in order, made from `screenplay-final.md` by `tools/shotlist/final_build.py`. **Plan only: nothing has been rendered for this script.** The times are estimates for an animatic; the real length is known only once an animatic is timed (`SERIES.md` section 1).

- **726 shots**: 331 from the handoff shot list (`../shotlist.json`, with the final script's changes), 352 in the proposed additions, 43 in the proposed rating pass. Everything proposed is in the script by default and is not canon until Daxtyn accepts it.
- **Estimated length: 54:14 expected** (tight cut 44:08, loose cut 68:50), 78096 frames at 24 fps. The shots use `tools/check_final.py`'s timing tables, which estimate 54:22; the few seconds between the two are the duplicate beats played once (1B-11, X7-drop) and rounding each shot to half a second.
- **385 spoken lines**, each placed in exactly one shot (ids from `dialogue.json`).
- **167 hard shots** (difficulty 4-5). **452 shots carry a realism strategy** (faces, crowds, blood, gore, the dead).
- Dropped as duplicates: 1B-11 (played by X3); X7-drop (played by 1F-05).
- Full detail for every shot (lighting, lens, positions, continuity flags, realism strategy, render cost) is in `shotlist.json`. The render plan is `render-schedule.md`.

## Continuity flags used

- **ABBY_BOTH_HANDS_BEFORE_PASS** (6 shots): Before the pass Abby uses both hands normally (set-up for the change).
- **ABBY_LEFT_ARM_INJURY_EVENT** (1 shots): The pass wrenches Abby's LEFT arm. Do not mirror the shot.
- **ABBY_LEFT_ARM_INJURED** (76 shots): Abby's LEFT arm is injured: held in, never used normally. Uses her right hand.
- **ABBY_SLING_LEFT** (26 shots): Abby's LEFT arm is in a sling (from 2C onward).
- **SCOUT_LEFT_WING_LOST** (7 shots): The scout lost its LEFT wing. Never show the right wing missing.
- **SCOUT_RIDER_UNIDENTIFIED** (5 shots): Scout rider stays unidentified: no face, insignia, or speech.
- **NO_WEAPON_STRIKE** (1 shots): The fracture comes from the pass and its wake, not from a weapon, bite, fall, or projectile.
- **BURNED_RIDER_ALIVE** (8 shots): The burned rider is alive when last seen: strapped on, moving.
- **NO_DEATH_DECLARED** (5 shots): Nobody declares the scout or its rider dead in Episode 1.
- **STARLIGHT_NO_WOUNDS** (18 shots): Starlight has NO wounds (no later-episode wing injuries).
- **STARLIGHT_NO_FIRE** (18 shots): Starlight never breathes fire in this episode. Damage comes only from the dive, contact, and her wake.
- **STARLIGHT_SIZE** (1 shots): Starlight is roughly twice Charcoal's size. Reflective white scales; no glow, no literal crystal.
- **ATTACK_NO_FLAME** (3 shots): "Attack" means a physical attack. It never triggers flame.
- **FIRE_ONLY_ON_FIRE_COMMAND** (1 shots): Charcoal's flame happens only after "Fire", and it is brief.
- **CHARCOAL_NOT_FASTER** (14 shots): Charcoal is never faster than the scout in a straight line. He closes through height, angles and the scout's turns.
- **LEAF_INITIATES_NIP** (1 shots): Leaf starts it with one quick, harmless nip.
- **LEAF_UNHARMED** (3 shots): Leaf is unharmed by the head-in-mouth beat. Teeth never close through skin.
- **SIZE_DISPARITY** (33 shots): Leaf's body is about a quarter of Charcoal's length (DRAGONS.md 2026-10-09; Charcoal ~36 m). Use long lenses so a foreground Leaf does not cheat the scale.
- **HATCHLING_NO_GLOW** (23 shots): The gold hatchling reflects light but never emits it. It is weak and has no name.
- **ORIGINAL_LINE_EXACT** (4 shots): An [ORIGINAL] line: use the exact wording.
- **ABBY_SILENCE** (2 shots): Abby does not answer the succession question: no words, no nod, no narration.
- **SCREEN_DIRECTION** (9 shots): Flight line: travel screen LEFT to RIGHT, camera on the inland side. Leaf LEFT/near and slightly behind, Charcoal RIGHT/far and ahead. Island on the inland side, open sea beyond them. Because they travel screen-right, this camera sees the dragons' and riders' RIGHT sides; their LEFT sides face seaward, away from camera.
- **SPEAKING_DISTANCE** (12 shots): Airborne/field speakers are close enough to hear each other. Wind sits between the lines; no shouted conversations across impossible distances.
- **REMI_CHECKS_ABBY_FIRST** (1 shots): Remi confirms Abby is secured and Leaf is stable before he pursues.
- **FIELD_CLEAR** (3 shots): The launch field is cleared. No bystanders are endangered for spectacle.
- **CLING_ROUTES** (23 shots): Cling map from the king's steps: stone arch LEFT, gate to broad road RIGHT, vendor stall left-center with alley behind, music space right-center, fountain with a central stone pillar at the center (the reference landmark), a separate stone support by the steps. Keep these fixed through the attack. Any reverse angle must be declared in camera_move and mirror these positions.
- **TRAITOR_IS_SUSPICION_ONLY** (1 shots): Traitor is a suspicion, not a confirmed identity or motive.
- **SCOUT_OWN_DESIGN** (28 shots): The scout is a small, very fast dragon of its own design (no species, DRAGONS.md 2026-10-09): two legs, wings as forelimbs, horns.
- **LOW_STANCE** (101 shots): Dragon on the ground: low and level, belly near the ground, two legs folded or standing under the MIDDLE of the body, folded wings' wrists touching down in front. No dog-sit on the haunches, no upright or rearing pose (DRAGONS.md 2026-10-16).
- **REMI_BLOOD_1** (17 shots): The scout's blood on Remi from the bite (1F): spattered and streaked on face, neck, hands and sleeves; none of it his. Same pattern in every shot until R8.
- **REMI_BLOOD_2** (23 shots): After R8 (keeper's cloth): face mostly scrubbed, streaks left at the hairline and down the neck; hands and sleeves still dark.
- **REMI_BLOOD_3** (34 shots): After X11 (his mother wipes hairline, neck and hands) and R9b (hands scrubbed at the healer's bowl): hands clean, red from scrubbing; sleeves stained for the rest of the day.
- **CHARCOAL_JAW_BLOOD** (15 shots): The scout's blood on Charcoal's lower jaw and throat from the bite until Remi washes it off in X15: wet at first, drying dark. Charcoal has no wound of his own.
- **VILLAGER_INJURIES** (6 shots): Adult villager: split scalp (blood down one side of the face into the collar) and an open fracture of the RIGHT leg below the knee, from the second pass to the end; leg splinted with a banner pole in X27. He lives.
- **NO_CHILD_IN_FRAME** (23 shots): Drink, smoke, drug or suggestive beat: no child (and not Abby or Remi) anywhere in frame (SERIES.md section 8).
- **DEAD_ARE_ADULTS** (9 shots): Every dead body is an adult; partly covered or turned away, no faces, no lingering close-up.
- **PENDING_WORDING** (3 shots): A line here keeps a scrapped dragon name until Daxtyn chooses new wording (DRAGONS.md): record it as written; plan a pickup for the alternative.
