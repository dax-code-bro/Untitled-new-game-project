# Episode 1, longer version: what we propose to add

**Everything in this file is a suggestion. None of it is canon.** Nothing here becomes part of Dragon's Kingdom until you, Daxtyn, say yes. You can say yes or no to each piece separately.

## The short version

- **You asked for episodes of 50 to 60 minutes.** Your words on 6 October 2026 were "most episodes only range 50 minutes to an hour." Episode 1 as currently written runs about **25 minutes** when told at a natural pace (`shotlist.json`).
- **The screenplay says not to stretch it.** That means no repeated shots, no frozen pictures and no slow talking. So the only honest way to make it longer is to add **new story**.
- **We wrote 28 new pieces**, called **X1 to X28**. Each one sits in the story at the moment it would happen.
- **With all 28, the episode is about 50½ minutes** (expected 50:36). That is just above your 50-minute floor. The real length is only known once we make the animatic, the rough test version timed with temporary voices. The estimate's outer bounds are 41:12 and 64:10 (see "How long the episode becomes").
- **This is the second draft.** A review found repeats, recaps and a few things that would decide later episodes' story. We took those out and added new story in their place. The first draft's estimate was 53:36. This one is shorter, but nothing in it is filler.
- **The current screenplay is not changed.** Every original line is still there, word for word and in the same order. That includes your four exact lines marked [ORIGINAL]. A checking script proves it (see the end of this file).
- **Where to read them:** `screenplay-extended.md`. New parts sit between a line `[PROPOSED ADDITION X# — begin]` and its matching `— end]` line. Everything outside those lines is the current screenplay.
- **Nothing new has been drawn or rendered yet.** This is writing only.

## What the new parts are about

- **Verdor feels like a real island:**
  - the harbor and town seen from the air;
  - fishermen working with common Nightwings;
  - a riding school for young riders;
  - the Santa Maria and her lookout at the quay before anyone needs them;
  - the harbor seeing the princess come home alone.
- **Abby and Remi:**
  - more teasing;
  - a race;
  - Abby's bravery when her arm is set ("Don't count. Just do it.");
  - an ending where Remi, who asked her the question in 1D, now can't answer *her* question.
- **Leaf and Charcoal as characters:**
  - in the morning Abby checks Leaf over;
  - at the end of the day Leaf turns so that she can still do it with one hand;
  - Leaf's cheekiness and pride;
  - Charcoal's patience;
  - the keepers suddenly careful around Charcoal's jaws after the fight.
- **Alexandria in private:**
  - she waited up all night for the egg;
  - she asks Remi to keep Abby where he can see her;
  - she feeds the weak hatchling with a cloth, and later watches it stand;
  - she has a quiet talk with each of her children.
- **A smarter chase.** Charcoal is never the faster flier. He gains only by diving from height he has paid for, by Remi knowing the rocks, and by hearing the scout inside the mist.
- **After the fight:**
  - Abby visits Leaf "later, with help". This pays off the screenplay's own "Later. With help." and "You should tell him." / "I will."
  - The Santa Maria sails out. We never show the rescue.
- **Cling before and after the attack:**
  - We visit Cling twice earlier in the day and meet its people: the vendor and the musician, the parent and child, the watchman, a farm family, and an old woman who teaches the children a dance. The attack hurts more because we know them.
  - Afterwards, the guard who promised "I'll look" finds the injured villager's family alive.
